What happens to your report?

Reports are saved for owner review. They contain the hardware, model, outcome and any optional settings, measurements, public name or notes you supply.

The original pasted output or file stays in your browser. The form extracts performance fields and sends those, along with the details visible in the form. The Ollama helper never uploads results automatically.

Approved reports and any public review notes appear in the results. Pending or held-back reports are visible only in the owner review queue. Do not include private information in public notes.

A random browser identifier supports retries. A salted hash of the request's network address is kept privately to limit abuse and avoid treating repeat reports from one source as independent evidence. Raw addresses are not stored in the submissions table.

Your unfinished form can be saved on this device so you can recover it after a reload or failed submission. Successful submission clears that draft.

The sandbox stores reports separately from the live Finder. Nothing updates recommendations automatically. You can ask the site owner to hold back a report, using the short reference shown after submission.
