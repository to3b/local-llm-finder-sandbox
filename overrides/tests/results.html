How these results are used

A review checks the supplied evidence and settings. “Reproduced” means the owner repeated the test. A contributor’s “ran successfully” report is an observation, not a measured quality score.

Approved repeatable benchmarks can inform calibration candidates. Changes to the Finder still require checking and a separate release.
